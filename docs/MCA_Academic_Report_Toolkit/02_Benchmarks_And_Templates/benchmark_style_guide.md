# Autonomous College Format & Style Specifications

This toolkit implements the official project documentation guidelines of **Amal Jyothi College of Engineering Autonomous, Kanjirappally** (Department of Computer Applications, Course `24MCAR295`):

---

### 1. Page Geometry & Margins
- **Paper Size**: International A4 (8.27 in × 11.69 in / 210 mm × 297 mm)
- **Left Margin (Binding Margin)**: `1.18 in` (30 mm)
- **Right Margin**: `1.0 in` (25.4 mm)
- **Top Margin**: `1.0 in` (25.4 mm)
- **Bottom Margin**: `1.0 in` (25.4 mm)
- **Header Distance**: `0.5 in` (12.7 mm)
- **Footer Distance**: `0.5 in` (12.7 mm)

---

### 2. Typography & Text Hierarchy
- **Body Font**: Times New Roman, Regular, 12 pt
- **Line Spacing**: 1.5 Lines
- **Paragraph Spacing**: `0 pt` Before, `6 pt` After
- **Alignment**: Fully Justified
- **Chapter Title (Master Report)**: Times New Roman, Bold, 18 pt, ALL CAPS, centered vertically on its own dedicated chapter-title page
- **Heading 1**: Times New Roman, Bold, 14 pt, Left-aligned (`14 pt` Before, `6 pt` After)
- **Heading 2**: Times New Roman, Bold, 13 pt, Left-aligned (`12 pt` Before, `4 pt` After)
- **Heading 3**: Times New Roman, Bold Italic, 12 pt, Left-aligned (`10 pt` Before, `3 pt` After)

---

### 3. Tables & Figures
- **Borders**: Clean single black solid border (`w:sz="4"`, color `#000000`). No colorful or dotted outlines.
- **Header Row**: Times New Roman, Bold, 10 pt, Centered, No background color shading (pure academic print).
- **Cell Padding**: `3 pt` Before/After, `1.15` line spacing, cells vertically centered.
- **Captions**:
  - Tables: Placed ABOVE table (`Table X.Y: Title`), Times New Roman, 10.5 pt, Bold, Centered.
  - Figures: Placed BELOW image (`Figure X.Y: Caption`), Times New Roman, 9.5 pt, Bold Italic, Centered.

---

### 4. Running Headers & Footers
- **Running Header**:
  - Left: Project Short Name / Title (Times New Roman, Bold, 9 pt, Dark Gray)
  - Right: Chapter Title or Sub-Report Category (Times New Roman, 8.5 pt, Muted Gray)
  - Border: 0.5 pt thin bottom divider line (`#888888`)
- **Running Footer**:
  - Left: "Amal Jyothi College of Engineering Autonomous, Kanjirappally" (Times New Roman, 8.5 pt)
  - Right: "Dept. of Computer Applications" (Times New Roman, 8.5 pt)
  - Border: 0.5 pt thin top divider line (`#888888`)
